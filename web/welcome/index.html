Before you start

A few useful answers.

Tenderpad helps you decide what to investigate. Your review stays central.

Does a high score mean I can bid?

A match score helps order opportunities by relevance. Eligibility, capacity and requirements still need to be checked against the official tender documents. Missing information stays unknown.

Can I try it before looking up my company?

Yes. The example workspace uses a fictional company and three fictional notices. You can inspect the evidence, try the filters and build a demo shortlist separately from your real company.

Where do my shortlist and notes go?

Saved opportunities, personal notes, assessments and next actions stay in this browser, separately for each company. Follow-up dates appear in the app; they do not send notifications.

Are the underlying documents always current?

The workspace shows retained records. Checking a saved notice compares it with the stored record; it does not refresh the official source. Confirm current deadlines and requirements before preparing a bid.
